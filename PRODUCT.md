# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The site is for anyone interested in its topics. It will also be presented as an informal school project.

## Product Purpose

Introduce visitors to artificial intelligence, influencers, and how these subjects are changing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a static web project with an editorial homepage, an interactive visual map, and article pages. Additional school-work sections and a separate photo gallery are being prepared. Their final texts, selected cybercrimes, team member names, and project photographs have not been provided and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

The project is named “Más allá del feed.” Its existing Liquid Chrome Glass visual identity, dark glass surfaces, and chrome-like framing are to be preserved.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing project files include the homepage, an article demonstration, an interactive map page, a logo, and a hero photograph. No team member names or gallery photographs were found.

## Product Principles

- Make the topics easy for a general audience to explore.
- Preserve supplied project information and distinguish unfinished work from published content.
- Keep navigation clear across desktop and mobile.
